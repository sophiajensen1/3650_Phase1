## Pain Points & supporting quotes
1. **Booking an appointment is limited and slow.**
   - "The closest DMV office that even showed up was in Ogden… I know there is an office in Logan." (P3)
   - "It was just a lot of reading and the verification of being a human is a little annoying." (P1)
   - Only 6 offices take appointments; the site never explains why a nearby office is missing.
2. **Vocabulary is difficult to follow.**
   - "MVP" on the appointment button: "I didn't know what it meant or if it was the right place." (P3)
   - "It just took me a second to find out that it was a title transfer is what was needed." (P1)
   - "Some of the language was a bit confusing… my vehicle is owned, not leased." (P3)
3. **Some fields lack clear instructions.**
   - "I didn't notice it said last 8 digits of my VIN number… I quickly realized when it wasn't letting me type more digits in." (P3)
4. **Cluttered homepage.**
   - "It's kind of cluttered, and has the same things in more than one place." (P3)
5. **Minor:** lag (P1), color (P2).

## Mental Model mismatches
Any nearby office can be booked > Only 6 offices accept appointmenets
"Moving to Utah" as a task label > under "title transfer"
One clear spot to book > Same link appears 3 times labeled as "MVP"
Type full VIN > field only accepts 8 

## Limitations to note
small sample group (3), single age group (25-34), self-reported.
